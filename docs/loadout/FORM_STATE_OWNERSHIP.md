## Incapacity và recovery recommendation

HP0 source trước switch commit hủy old request, close future unreleased actions, clear queue. Under B, reserve HP0 disabled giữ progression; healthy viable equipped member có thể emergency transfer theo R03 one defeatEventId, không cost gate/iframe/heal, vẫn recovery/CD. Đây là proposal cần L16-U05, không manual miễn phí. Mọi equipped form unsuitable/dead/unavailable thì end attempt rồi safe checkpoint recovery, không auto equip ngoài frozen set.

Boss loss reset boss+attempt transients trước recovery/unlock; retained earned durable progression/grants không xóa, no first-clear từ failed attempt theo P15. Checkpoint heal/resource/CD refill chỉ approved recovery transaction, no menu/reload refill với boss damage cũ. New unlock sau defeat vào collection, không auto revive/replace active; sau abort/reset/unlock mới preparation/equip eligible form. Nếu không viable playable available thì preserve save, validated recovery state/last-good boundary và report content gap, không manufacture species. Ordinary defeat không permanent species loss, không lore resurrection mới.
