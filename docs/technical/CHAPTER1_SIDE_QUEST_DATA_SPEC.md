## Source aliases và derived truth

S05 optional quest/source IDs chưa có concrete optional event bindings trước Task22. Proposed bindings là definitions trong cùng W07 catalog, completion/source view phải map một nghĩa; canonical reward claim vẫn optional logical source/target/component. Không tạo cả legacy optional completion và optional event completion độc lập. Actual source/alias/recipient/stacking mapping cần duyệt S22-U04 trước implementation; schema names không chọn storage engine.

Main quest completion/consequences vẫn source S05/Q21; side effects chỉ local permission domain, no side proof substituting E03/E04/E05/E06 or boss capability. Zone/connector refs đúng H19, six wildlife ref_i_* chỉ external drafts đến official mapping/approval, `quy_s1/s2/s3` stage/ability refs typed. Proposed mem_quy_refuge_margin không main first_sense/current hoặc approved lore chỉ vì new ID collision-free.

## Execution/lifecycle requirements tương lai

Shared objective adapter nhận command→source predicate/route/cap/context validation→candidate optional outcome+owned patch/evidence/memory/knowledge+P15 recipient/ledger+coverage/placement→durable W07 commit→derived quest/UI/cues. Sources already-done query prior valid proof, no actor event phải phát lại/accept trước. Source unavailable/altered use approved equivalent/retrospective/archive theo loại, no fake living source. Replay and read-only lightweight activities không trigger reward writer.

Five side-facing statuses + shared transient READY/overlays ở [availability](../chapter1/CHAPTER1_SIDE_QUEST_AVAILABILITY.md) là views của Q21 framework, không new engine. Existing acceptance/selected objective có thể lưu nếu policy cần, completion at source. Mất focus/pause clear held input, transient challenge không offline fail/hit/reward. Boss prep/active contexts check L16 before optional mutation/recovery; abandon không unlock/replace members.

## Validation/migration gates

All references unique/type/approval; main/side DAG acyclic và no optional→main prerequisite; start/objective/return reachability với available guaranteed cap/tier; pre/post E05 coverage incl undiscovered; source/recipient payload identity frozen; partial-save last-good và one owner/property; localization/art readiness. Relative complexity thấp không cho skip content approval.

New content version chỉ pin data/migrate, no reward re-open. Deferred playable approval không retro grant form vì knowledge fallback đã claimed; explicit legitimate entitlement/source/migration policy riêng required. Removed quest/reward/source giữ earned records và safe pending-source resolution, không delete save/alias guessed. Production runtime classes/AI/maps/JSON và save backend không được viết/chọn ở Task22.
