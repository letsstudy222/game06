## Test strategy sau approval

Unit/model/property checks: membership/subset/selection/body-count, eligibility/typed aliases, cancel/debt/state graph, ordered damage/commit, clocks/status/source attribution. Integration/fault injection: entry lock, completion/reset/close/retry, old save/migration/invalid placement, W07/P15 atomic receipts. Browser input/playback: keyboard/focus/visibility/gamepad disconnect/touchcancel/resize; actual second-species geometry/status/animation/small-screen readability tách khỏi one-form fixture. Physical device/performance/playtest balance là evidence riêng, chưa có. Báo passed/failed/skipped/unrun rõ, không zero-test hoặc old Phoenix test proof. Task16 hiện chỉ local doc validation, không comprehensive PhaseII audit.
